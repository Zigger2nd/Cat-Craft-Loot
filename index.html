How to Play

Welcome to Cat Clicker! Take care of your cat and collect treats.

Collect Treats

Click your cat to earn treats. Each click gives you treats according to your current treats-per-click level.

Buy Upgrades

Purchase clicker upgrades to earn more treats with every click.

Cat Generators

Buy toys, bowls, and beds to generate treats automatically over time. Each generator produces a different amount of treats per second.

Open Loot Boxes

Use energy to open loot boxes and collect cat skins. Duplicate skins can reward you with dust.

Collect Skins & Backgrounds

View your collected skins in My Skins and visit the Shop to unlock new backgrounds.

Daily Rewards

Check the Daily List regularly to claim your rewards.
